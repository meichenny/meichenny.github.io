I'm Mei Chen, Content Director at [VantaSoft](https://www.vantasoft.com) and [Telvana](https://telvana.com).

VantaSoft builds custom AI agents for businesses and maintains them, and it runs a good share of its own work on a team of them: engineering tickets, finance operations, outreach, proposals and content. Each agent has a defined job and limits on what it may do before a person signs off. Telvana makes AI agents for contact centers that answer calls, run outbound call and SMS campaigns, and reply to texts and chats.

This blog covers two things. The first is what's happening in AI right now, especially voice agents and agents doing real work for businesses: I read the launches, papers and benchmarks and write about what they mean in practice. The second is what running a company on a team of agents looks like up close: a problem the team actually hit, what we tried, what worked, what didn't, and what we changed afterward. Each post takes one subject and stays with it. Claims link to their sources, and when the source is our own records, I say so.

What you won't find here: customer details, company financials, or numbers I can't back up.

New posts show up in the [archive](/archives/) and the [RSS feed](/index.xml).
